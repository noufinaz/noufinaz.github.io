Solar installations proceed only after verifying KSEB technical feasibility, local distribution transformer (DTR) balance capacity (90% DTR / 30% per-phase KSERC cap), and sanctioned connected load. Single-phase connections typically support up to 3 kW (or 5 kW where three-phase is unavailable).
